# Templater
[Github](https://github.com/SilentVoid13/Templater)
![[templater_logo.svg]]
> Create and use templates.

> [!warning] 
> This plugin is **not** the same as a WikiMedia template, where a change on the template will affect all instances. They act more as stencils that output plain text that you can further customize.
