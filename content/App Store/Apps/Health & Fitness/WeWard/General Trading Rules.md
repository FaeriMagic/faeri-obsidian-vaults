# General Trading Guidelines

**Terms:**
- *Ephemerals [E]* are cards that are only available for a limited period before being unobtainable from card packs.
- *Vintages [V]* are cards that are unobtainable from card packs.
- *Recents [R]* are normal card sets that are currently in demand. I personally limit the Recents to the five most recently released card sets.
- *Soon Vintages [SV]* are normal card sets that will soon be unobtainable from card packs.
- *Normals [N]* are normal cards sets that are neither Recents nor Soon Vintages that are currently obtainable from card packs.
- *Randoms* are any random cards. I allow substituting Normals with Randoms if that is easier for you. (Rarity must still match.)

**Note:**
- These guidelines aren't fixed. I'd say these guidelines are more "Value-oriented" rather than "Community-oriented". When I do "community-oriented" trades, I'm much more willing to agree to more generous trades to help others complete their collections.
- When offering \[Common] cards for \[Common] Normals, the card number is how much it is worth in \[Common]s. For example, a Normal Card \# 4 is worth 4 \[Common]s.
