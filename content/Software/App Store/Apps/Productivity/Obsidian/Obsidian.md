Obsidian is a powerful knowledge base that works on top of a local folder of plain text Markdown files.

It is a second brain, for you, forever. Now available on the go for iOS!

Features include:
- [[App Store/Apps/Productivity/Obsidian/Obsidian Settings#Ribbon menu configuration|Customizable toolbar]]
- Pull down quick actions
- Graph view
- [[Plugins old|Community plugins]]
- [[Themes]]
- Sidebar pinning for tablet
- iCloud vaults
