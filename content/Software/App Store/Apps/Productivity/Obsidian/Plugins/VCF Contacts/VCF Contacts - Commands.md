## VCF Contacts: Create Contact
Creates a new contact note in your vault using the VCF (vCard) format, prompting you to fill in contact details such as name, email, and phone number. Requires the VCF community plugin.
## VCF Contacts: Open Contacts Sidebar
Opens the contacts sidebar panel, displaying all contact notes in your vault for quick browsing and access. Requires the VCF Contacts community plugin.
