**_[[AFK Journey]]_ is** an astonishingly deep roleplaying game, with a mighty roster of fully realized characters—each with a comprehensive backstory—and an ambitious narrative layered with rich lore. By the time we finished the first chapter, the city of Holistone’s cobblestone streets felt as familiar as one of our childhood haunts.

But _AFK Journey_ is also as innovative as it is deep. While traveling through the quaint towns, verdant wildernesses, windblown deserts, and tropical islands of Esperia as Magister Merlin, we were struck by how _AFK Journey_ streamlines the genre in brilliant new ways.

Handy teleportation Waystones and auto-navigation had us zipping through quests. Boosting stats for entire classes of characters is accomplished in one fell swoop. Let your squad of heroes tussle automatically, or unleash fight-changing ultimates as you see fit. And because heroes level up together, experimenting with new tactics is unintimidating and instantly rewarding.

True to its mobile roots, _AFK Journey_ also makes it easy to dive back into its world, no matter how much (or little) time we have to play. Starting a long bus commute? Return to the ever-expanding campaign, filled with sidequests, mini-games, and slick cutscenes. Have a few minutes in the grocery line? Tackle a Dream Realm boss fight to rake in resources, or climb the Graveborn Tower of Eternity in the lucrative Legend Trials.

_AFK Journey_ even doled out rewards when we were doing nothing at all: Log back in after a couple of hours and—voilà!—buckets of free treasure! (AFK stands for “away from keyboard,” after all.)

With countless entertainment options competing for our time, this “everything” game magically expands and contracts to fit seamlessly into our lives. It’s a journey we won’t soon forget.
