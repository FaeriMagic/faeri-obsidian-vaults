<p align="justify"><b>Throughout the year,</b> App Store Editors around the world search for the very best apps and games to recommend each day. The App Store Awards are a culmination of that expert curation—a chance to celebrate titles that improve people’s lives <i>and</i> showcase the highest levels of technical innovation, user experience, and design.

With so many outstanding apps and games on the App Store, selecting just a few is no easy task. We’re proud to honor this year’s winners. Congratulations to them all!

<i>Some titles may not be available to download in your region or on all devices.</i></p>
